# SaaS Sector Primer

## Key Dynamics
- **Recurring revenue model:** ARR, NRR, churn are critical
- **High gross margins:** 70% standard, 75% is strong
- **Long sales cycles:** 3-6 months typical
- **Unit economics matter:** CAC, LTV, payback period

## Financial Metrics
| Metric | Formula | Good | Best |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARR | Annual Recurring Revenue | >$1m | >$5m |
| NRR | (ARR_end + expansion - churn) / ARR_start | >100% | >110% |
| CAC Payback | (CAC) / (ARPU * Gross Margin) | <12 mo | <9 mo |
| Magic Number | (New ARR * 4) / S&M Spend | >0.5 | >0.75 |
| Gross Margin | (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue | >70% | >75% |

## Value Creation Levers
1. **Land:** Accelerate new customer acquisition (sales productivity)
2. **Expand:** Increase NRR through upsells (product expansion)
3. **Efficiency:** Reduce CAC through better targeting/ops
4. **Retention:** Minimize churn through product/customer success
5. **Pricing:** Capture more value from customers (price increase, packaging)

## PE Perspective
- Look for 30%+ growth + 110% NRR = high quality
- Churn <2%/month is acceptable, <1%/month is excellent
- CAC payback <12 months = efficient sales model
- Magic number >0.75 = strong sales efficiency
