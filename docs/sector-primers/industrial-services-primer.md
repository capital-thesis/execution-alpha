# Industrial Services Sector Primer

## Key Dynamics
- **Sticky, long-term contracts:** customer relationships are valuable
- **Service-based pricing:** can be fee-for-service, hourly, or outcome-based
- **Labor quality matters:** skilled technicians = differentiation
- **Consolidation opportunity:** fragmented, family-owned businesses

## Financial Metrics
| Metric | Typical | Good |
|---|---|---|
| EBITDA Margin | 10-15% | 18%+ |
| Contract Length | 2-3 years | 5+ years |
| Customer Retention | >90% | >95% |
| Repeat Revenue | >80% | >90% |
| Labor Cost Ratio | 45-55% of revenue | <45% of revenue |

## Value Creation Levers
1. **Pricing:** increase prices on contract renewals (+2-4%)
2. **Labor Mix:** shift to subcontractors, reduce overhead
3. **Service Expansion:** add high-margin services to existing customers
4. **Operational Efficiency:** process automation, headcount optimization
5. **Organic Growth:** land new customers, expand geographies

## PE Perspective
- Sticky, recurring revenue = lower risk, higher valuation
- Service quality = differentiation = pricing power
- Labor efficiency = margin expansion = value creation
- Consolidation = roll-up opportunity
