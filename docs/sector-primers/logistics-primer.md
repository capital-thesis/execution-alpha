# Logistics Sector Primer

## Key Dynamics
- **Asset-light or asset-intensive:** impacts capex and margins
- **Labor-intensive:** headcount productivity is critical
- **Fragmented market:** consolidation opportunity
- **Pricing power:** dependent on service differentiation

## Financial Metrics
| Metric | Typical | Good |
|---|---|---|
| EBITDA Margin | 12-15% | 18% |
| Headcount Productivity | £150-200k per FTE | £250k+ per FTE |
| Asset Turnover | 2-3x | 3-4x |
| Customer Retention | >90% | >95% |
| Utilization Rate | 70-80% | 85% |

# Value Creation Levers
1. **Pricing:** +2-5% rate increases on renewals
2. **Labor Optimization:** shift to subcontractors, automation
3. **Procurement:** vendor consolidation, fuel hedging
4. **Headcount Productivity:** reduce management layers, improve utilization
5. **Organic Growth:** land new customers, expand into new geographies

## PE Perspective
- Sticky contracts (3-5 year terms) reduce risk
- Service differentiation = pricing power
- Headcount productivity = margin expansion opportunity
- Fragmentation = consolidation play
