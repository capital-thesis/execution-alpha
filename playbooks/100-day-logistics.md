# 100-Day Operational Playbook: Logistics Services

**Case Study:** Logistics Services LBO
**Hold Period:** 5 years | **IRR Target:** 20.8% | **EBITDA Target:** £52.5m

---

## Phase 1: Operational Baseline (Days 1-14)

### Key Metrics to Establish
| Metric | Current | Target | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | £200m | £350m | Finance |
| EBITDA Margin | 15% | 20%+ | Finance |
| Cost per Shipment | £X | £X-3% | Ops |
| On-time Delivery Rate | ? | >97% | Ops |
| Headcount Productivity | £200k/FTE | £250k/FTE | HR |

### Activities
1. Financial review: margin trends, driver analysis
2. Operational walkthroughs: fleet, network, headcount
3. Customer interviews: top 20 customers (pricing power, stickiness)
4. Procurement audit: vendor concentration, renegotiation opportunities
5. Headcount analysis: role productivity, redundancies

### Deliverable
- 40-slide operational baseline deck

---

## Phase 2: Diagnostics (Days 15-30)

### Operational Levers
- **Pricing:** Can we push rates +3-5%? Customer WTP by segment?
- **Procurement:** Route optimization, fuel hedging, vendor consolidation
- **Headcount:** Elimination of duplicate roles, automation opportunities
- **Network:** Hub efficiency, consolidation opportunities

### Deliverable
- Deep-dive: pricing analysis, procurement savings, headcount optimization

---

## Phase 3: Value Creation Roadmap (Days 31-60)

### EBITDA Bridge
**Entry EBITDA:** £30m → **Exit EBITDA:** £52.5m (75% uplift)

| Lever | Impact | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Organic Growth | +£10m | 5% annual growth on expanded base |
| Pricing | +£4m | +3% price increase on low-elasticity routes |
| COGS Efficiency | +£5m | Procurement, route optimization, fuel savings |
| OpEx Leverage | +£3.5m | Headcount productivity, SG&A rationalization |

---

## Phase 4: Execution (Days 61-100)

### Quick Wins
- [ ] Renegotiate top 3 vendor contracts (-£1m COGS)
- [ ] Implement pricing increase on low-elasticity routes (+£1m EBITDA)
- [ ] Consolidate regional hubs, eliminate headcount (-£0.5m OpEx)
- [ ] Launch fuel hedging program (+£0.3m EBITDA)

**Total Quick Win: +£1.8m EBITDA (~6% uplift)**
