# 100-Day Operational Playbook: SaaS Platform

**Case Study:** SaaS Platform LBO  
**Hold Period:** 5 years | **IRR Target:** 22%+ | **EBITDA Target:** £45m

---

## Phase 1: Operational Baseline (Days 1–14)

### Objectives
- Build comprehensive operational baseline
- Identify quick wins
- Establish KPI tracking

### Key Metrics to Establish
| Metric | Current | Target | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue) | £15m | £45m | Finance |
| Net Revenue Retention (NRR) | ? | >110% | Product/Finance |
| Gross Margin | ? | >75% | Finance |
| CAC Payback Period | ? | <12 months | Sales |
| Customer Churn Rate | ? | <2%/month | Product |

### Activities
1. **Financial review:** Validate last 3 years actuals, forecast accuracy
2. **Operational walkthroughs:** Meet all function leads
3. **Customer interviews:** Speak with top 10 customers (NPS, churn risk)
4. **Competitive analysis:** Map competitive positioning
5. **Data audit:** What's tracked? What's missing?

### Deliverable
- 40-slide operational baseline deck

---

## Phase 2: Diagnostics & Deep-Dives (Days 15–30)

### Revenue & Growth
- Analyze sales pipeline coverage
- Assess sales productivity (revenue per rep, ramp time)
- Map customer retention: churn by cohort, expand rates
- Pricing audit: willingness to pay by segment

### Gross Margin
- COGS breakdown by product/segment
- Service delivery efficiency
- Vendor concentration risk
- Procurement opportunities

### OpEx Efficiency
- Headcount by function (sales, marketing, R&D, G&A)
- Cost per headcount vs. revenue
- G&A rationalization opportunities

### Deliverable
- Deep-dive presentations (1 per function): diagnosis + top 3 opportunities + financial impact

---

## Phase 3: Value Creation Roadmap (Days 31–60)

### KPI Cascade
**Entry EBITDA:** £15m → **Exit EBITDA:** £45m (100% uplift)

| Lever | Target | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **Land Growth** | ARR +£15m | New customer acquisition (target: 50 new logos/year) |
| **Expand** | ARR +£10m | Upsell/cross-sell existing base (target: 15% expansion revenue) |
| **Margins** | Improve 5% | Service efficiency, pricing optimization |
| **Churn** | <1.5%/month | Product improvements, customer success |

### Workstreams (Pick Top 4)
1. **Land Expansion** (Owner: VP Sales) → Revenue +£15m
2. **Expand Motion** (Owner: VP CSM) → Revenue +£10m
3. **Pricing Optimization** (Owner: CFO) → Margin +3%
4. **Efficiency** (Owner: VP Ops) → OpEx -10%

### Deliverable
- 100-day roadmap + 5-year value creation waterfall

## Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Key customer churn | Medium | High | Weekly health reviews, exec sponsorship |
| Sales team turnover | Low | High | Retention bonuses locked in Day 1 |
| Pricing pushback | Medium | Medium | Segment pricing by WTP, grandfathering plan |
| Product disruption | Low | Medium | Roadmap transparency, comms cadence |
