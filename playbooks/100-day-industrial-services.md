# 100-Day Operational Playbook: Industrial Services

**Generic playbook for industrial service businesses**
**Hold Period:** 5 years | **IRR Target:** 18-22% | **Focus:** Operational leverage + pricing power

---

### Phase 1: Operational Baseline (Days 1-14)

### Key Metrics to Establish
| Metric | Current | Target | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | £Xm | £Xm | Finance |
| EBITDA Margin | X% | X%+ | Finance |
| Contract Stickiness | X years | X+ years | Sales |
| Headcount Productivity | £Xk/FTE | £Xk/FTE | HR |
| Customer Concentration | Top 10 = X% | <30% | Sales |

### Activites
1. Financial review: revenue stability, margin trends
2. Contract analysis: pricing structure, renewal rates, escalators
3. Customer interviews: top 15 customers (satisfaction, expansion)
4. Headcount analysis: productivity, skill gaps, turnover
5. Procurement audit: vendor concentration, labor costs

### Deliverable
- 40-slide operational baseline

---

## Phase 2: Diagnostics (Days 15-30)

### Key Questions
- How sticky are contracts? Renewal rates by cohort?
- What's the pricing model? Fixed fees, hourly, usage-based?
- Can we increase pricing on renewals?
- Where is headcount overstaffed?
- What's the service delivery model? Own labor vs. subcontractors?

### Deliverable
- Deep-dive on pricing power, labor optimization, retention

---

## Phase 3: Value Creation (Days 31-60)

### Typical Levers for Industrial Services
| Lever | Typical Impact | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing | +3-5% EBITDA | Annual price increases on renewals |
| Labor Mix | +2-3% EBITDA | Shift to subcontractors, automation |
| Headcount Productivity | +2% EBITDA | Reduce management layers |
| Revenue Growth | +3-5% EBITDA | Land new customers, expand existing |

---

## Phase 4: Execution (Days 61-100)

### Quick Wins
- [ ] Increase pricing on top 50% of contracts (+1-2% EBITDA)
- [ ] Implement labor optimization plan (-1% OpEx)
- [ ] Launch new service offering (+0.5% revenue)
- [ ] Reduce G&A headcount (-0.3% OpEx)
