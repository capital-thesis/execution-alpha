# SaaS Platform LBO - Case Summary

## Investment Thesis
Mid-market SaaS platform trading at 7.0x EBITDA. Strong NRR (115%+) and gross margins (75%+) provide multiple expansion and organic growth runway. PE can drive EBITDA from £15m → £45m through land expansion, pricing optimization, and operational leverage.

## Entry Valuation
- Entry EBITDA: £15.0m
- Entry Multiple: 7.0x
- Entry EV: £105m
- Debt: £80m
- Equity: £105m

## Exit Assumptions
- Exit EBITDA: £45.0m (30% CAGR growth)
- Exit Multiple: 9.0x (multiple expansion)
- Exit EV: £405m

## Value Creation Waterfall
| Lever | EBITDA Impact | %  |
|---|---|---|
| Organic Growth | +£20m | 67% |
| Margin Expansion | +£7m | 23% |
| Multiple Expansion | Portfolio | 10% |
| **Total Uplift** | **+£27m (180%)** | **100%** |

## Returns
- MoIC: 2.4x
- IRR: 22.5%

## Key Risks
- Sales team turnover (mitigate: locked retention)
- Pricing pushback (mitigate: segment pricing)
- Product competition (mitigate: NPS tracking)
