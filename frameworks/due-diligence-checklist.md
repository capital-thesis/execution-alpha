# Due Diligence Framework Checklist

## Financial DD
- [ ] Revenue stability: last 3 years trends, customer concentration
- [ ] Margin trends: gross margin, operating margin by segment
- [ ] Working capital: DSO, DPO, inventory turns
- [ ] Capital intensity: capex as % of revenue
- [ ] Debt covenants: leverage ratios, refinancing risk
- [ ] Tax structure: effective tax rate, jurisdictions

## Operational DD
- [ ] Team: key person risk, turnover rates, bench strength
- [ ] Customers: top 10 = what %? Churn by cohort? NPS?
- [ ] Suppliers: concentration risk, price elasticity, alternatives
- [ ] Processes: documented? Automation opportunities? SOP gaps?
- [ ] Systems: legacy or modern? Data quality? Cloud or on-prem?
- [ ] Facilities: efficiency, maintenance, lease/own structure

## Strategic DD
- [ ] TAM: market size, growth rate, addressable market
- [ ] Competition: direct competitors, indirect, new entrants
- [ ] Differentiation: IP, brand, switching costs, moat strength
- [ ] Pricing power: WTP by segment, price elasticity, pricing model
- [ ] Roadmap: product strategy, new markets, R&D pipeline

## Legal & Compliance
- [ ] Contracts: customer concentration, auto-renewal clauses
- [ ] Regulatory: licenses, compliance, pending regulation
- [ ] Litigation: active lawsuits, regulatory investigations
- [ ] Intellectual property: patents, trademarks, trade secrets
