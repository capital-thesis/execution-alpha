# KPI Tree Builder Framework

## Concept
Cascade financial targets down to operational KPIs by function and department

**Goal:** Link exit EBITDA target to weekly/monthly metrics that drive execution.

---

## Example: SaaS Platform

### Financial Target
- **Entry EBITDA:** £15m
- **Exit EBITDA:** £45m
- **Uplift Required:** £30m (200%)

### Revenue KPI Cascade (£30m uplift needed)

**Land: New Customer Acquisition**
- Target: +£15m new ARR
- Owner: VP Sales
- Breakdown:
  - 50 new logos/year
  - £300k average contract value (ACV)
  - 25% sales close rate
  - Sales productivity: £1.5m per rep
  - Weekly tracking: pipeline, deal velocity

**Expand: Existing Customer Upsell**
- Target: +£10m expansion revenue
- Owner: VP Customer Success
- Breakdown:
  - NRR target: 115%
  - Expansion revenue: 15% of cohort ARR
  - Product adoption: 40% of customers
  - Weekly tracking: NRR, churn, expansion revenue

### Margin KPI Cascade (+£5m from margin expansion)

**Gross Margin: 72% → 77% (+500 bps)**
- Owner: VP Operations
- COGS per customer: reduce 8%
- Support efficiency: reduce hours per customer 15%
- Weekly tracking: gross margin %, COGS per ARR

### OpEx KPI Cascade (-£5m from leverage)

**S&M Efficiency**
- Keep S&M spend flat while revenue doubles
- CAC payback: <12 months
- Magic number: >0.75

**G&A Leverage**
- Reduce G&A from 12% to 8% of revenue
- Consolidate tools, offshore functions
- Weekly tracking: opex as % of revenue

## Example: Logistics Services

### Financial Target
- **Entry EBITDA:** £30m
- **Exit EBITDA:** £52.5m
- **Uplift Required:** £22.5m (75%)

### Revenue KPI Cascade

**Organic Growth: 5% CAGR**
- Target: +£10m revenue
- Owner: VP Sales
- Mechanism: expand routes, new geographies
- Weekly tracking: revenue growth %, new routes

**Pricing: +3% rate increase**
- Target: +£4m revenue
- Owner: VP Sales
- Mechanism: annual escalations, strategic increases
- Weekly tracking: average rate per shipment

### Margin KPI Cascade

**EBITDA Margin: 15% → 20% (+500 bps)**
- Cost per shipment: -3%
- Fuel efficiency: -2%
- Labor productivity: +2%

### OpEx KPI Cascade

**Headcount Productivity: £200k → £250k per FTE**
- Reduce management layers
- Improve uitlization rates
- Weekly tracking: revenue per FTE

## Framework Template

### Step 1: Define Financial Target
Exit EBITDA Target: £XXm
Entry EBITDA: £XXm
Uplift Needed: £XXm

### Step 2: Cascade by Revenue/Margin/OpEx
Revenue Levers:
├─ Lever 1: +£Xm (Owner, weekly metric)
├─ Lever 2: +£Xm (Owner, weekly metric)
└─ Lever 3: +£Xm (Owner, weekly metric)

Margin Levers:
├─ Gross Margin: X% → Y% (Owner, metric)
└─ OpEx Leverage: X% → Y% (Owner, metric)

### Step 3: Assign Owners & Frequency
- Owner: who is accountable?
- Frequency: weekly, monthly, quarterly?
- Tracking: what metric proves progress?

### Step 4: Weekly Dashboard
Track all KPIs on a single dashboard that rolls up to EBITDA bridge.
